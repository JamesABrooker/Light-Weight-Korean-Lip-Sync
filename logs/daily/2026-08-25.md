## 2026-08-24: KMSAV Cropping Error - Issue 3

I have spent most of the day troubleshooting the KMSAV 
face cropping error. After analysing my cropped footage
I found that the bounding boxes zoomed in at a seemingly
random point on the video - not matching the face tracking
I expected.

I believe that this error has occurred because the given
bounding box annotations (ASD metadata) expects a fixed
1920 x 1080 frame resolution whereas
the videos I downloaded are at varying resolutions
(e.g. 1280 x 720), applying the raw pixel coordinates directly 
off-target shifted the crops into out-of-bounds frame areas.

I have
attempted a fix that upscales/normalises all the raw footage to 1920 
x 1080 prior to cropping. Currently re-running the pipeline again and seeing
if this fixes my problem, if not, other tests and experiments
will be made.
