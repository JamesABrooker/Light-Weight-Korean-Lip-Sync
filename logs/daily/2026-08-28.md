## 2026-08-27: Implementing manual check of dataset

Per last log, I found that a large portion of the data in my dataset is
unusable. By this, I mean that some clips don't show the speakers face,
the speaker isn't looking toward the camera (can't see their lips), or the
clip is in general low quality and would not help the models training.

To account for this, I wrote manual_data_check.py. This script plays every
clip that is listed in kmsav_pilot_manifest, and requests users input per
clip to either keep or discard the clip. All kept clips are added to a new
CSV file names kmsav_pilot_manifest_reviewed.csv.

The next step is to run this script and manually sort through all of the data.
